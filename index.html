How it works

  1. Check

    We review your email setup and sign-ins and look for the warning signs attackers leave behind. You get a written report in plain English.

  2. Lock down

    We close the gaps: multi-factor sign-in, blocks on impersonation and forwarding, and a wire-verification procedure your team can follow.

  3. Watch

    We keep an eye on risky sign-ins and new mailbox rules every business day, and send you a short report each month.
