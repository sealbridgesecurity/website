Who we help

Title agencies and brokerages

Wire fraud starts with a compromised or spoofed mailbox. Lenders and underwriters also expect the security controls in ALTA Best Practices Pillar 3.

  • Email and Microsoft 365 checked for the gaps attackers use
  • Written security program, privacy plan and incident plan
  • Wire-verification procedure and staff training
  • Evidence organized for your next Pillar 3 review

CPA and tax firms

Under the FTC Safeguards Rule, paid tax preparers must keep a written information security plan, and you acknowledge it each time you renew your PTIN.

  • Email and Microsoft 365 checked for the gaps attackers use
  • Written information security plan (WISP) built around how your office actually works
  • Yearly risk assessment and plan review
  • Staff training on phishing and client-data handling

We help you get ready and stay ready. Your assessor, auditor or insurer makes the final call; Sealbridge does not certify compliance.

How it works

  1. Check

    With your permission, we review your email and Microsoft 365 setup using read-only access and look for the warning signs attackers leave behind. You get a written report in plain English.

  2. Fix and document

    We work with your IT provider to close the gaps, write the plans your office needs and train your staff.

  3. Re-assess

    Each year, as a new engagement, we check again and update your plans before your next review or renewal.
